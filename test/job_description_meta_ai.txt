# Meta AI — Research Engineer / Deep Learning Systems
**Source:** Meta AI Careers / LinkedIn
**Location:** Menlo Park, CA / Remote (US)
**Domain:** Deep Learning, Large Language Models, PyTorch Systems

## About Meta AI
Meta AI Research develops state-of-the-art artificial intelligence models (such as Llama, Segment Anything, and PyTorch ecosystem technologies) to advance open science and empower billions of users worldwide.

## The Role
We are seeking a Research / Deep Learning Engineer to join our AI Research & Infrastructure team. You will work alongside research scientists to design, optimize, and train next-generation deep learning architectures, transformer foundation models, and distributed training systems.

## Key Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and benchmark state-of-the-art deep learning architectures using PyTorch, CUDA, and distributed computation frameworks.
- Optimize transformer pre-training, fine-tuning (LoRA, QLoRA), and inference acceleration pipelines.
- Build high-performance scientific simulations, stochastic data pipelines, and numerical algorithms in Python, Scala, C++, or Java.
- Scale multi-node distributed training infrastructure across GPU/TPU clusters, profiling memory consumption and throughput bottlenecks.
- Collaborate with research teams on scientific publications, open-source model releases, and algorithmic innovation.

## Qualifications & Requirements
- 4+ years of hands-on experience in machine learning engineering, deep learning research, or high-performance scientific computing.
- Deep expertise in PyTorch, neural network theory, attention mechanisms, and transformer architectures.
- Strong software engineering foundation in Python, C++, Java, or Scala.
- Demonstrated experience in distributed systems, model training optimization, and profiling GPU/memory performance.
- Master's or PhD degree in Computer Science, Applied Mathematics, Computational Biology, Physics, or equivalent technical experience.
